Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

What MekDaa stores during setup

When you supply a Google Drive folder link and initialize MekDaa, the assistant creates or reuses a MekDaa workspace in the Google Drive folder you select. Its configuration includes identifiers for the chosen root folder, the workspace folder, the sessions folder, and configuration/index documents; a generated installation ID; schema information; and setup and onboarding status. A session index is also created or reused so that saved records can be located later.

What MekDaa saves when you ask

Only when you request a save, MekDaa instructs ChatGPT to prepare a record from conversation context available to the assistant and write it into your Drive workspace. A session may contain its title, session ID, a detailed summary, important decisions, outstanding tasks, next actions, and notes about what context was available. The session index may include a title, session ID, summary, Drive document identifier or link, and a timestamp when available. A requested transcript includes only messages actually available or supplied to the assistant; it may be incomplete.

Where the data goes

MekDaa is a skills-only package, not a separate hosted data platform. Its current package does not include a publisher-operated session database, server, additional analytics/tracking code, or automatic conversation capture. Session records, workspace configuration and index files are stored in the user-selected Google Drive folder. The publisher does not receive automatic access to those files and does not keep separate copies or sell session data.

ChatGPT and the Google Drive integration you connect process requests under their respective service terms and privacy policies. If you share a Drive file or grant someone access to it, that person may be able to view the shared data.

Retention and deletion

MekDaa's package sets no automatic retention or deletion timer. You control the stored Drive files and their sharing permissions and can delete them using Google Drive. Google's own retention, trash, recovery, and account policies continue to apply. The publisher does not operate a separate retention, backup, or deletion service for user sessions.

Your choices and sensitive information

You choose which workspace to use and when to save. Check what you ask MekDaa to save, especially when a conversation involves sensitive data. Inspect the saved record afterward and read its coverage notes to understand what was retained. MekDaa instructs the assistant to omit raw passwords, access tokens, and credentials; do not rely on those instructions as a guarantee against all mistakes.

Support

Support is available through GitHub Issues. Issues posted to this public repository may be visible to everyone. Do not include personal session information, private Google Drive links, identifiers, tokens, or other confidential details.
