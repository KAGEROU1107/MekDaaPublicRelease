The service

MekDaa is a free, user-initiated ChatGPT skills package published by KAGEROU. It helps users save and recall currently available ChatGPT conversation context using a Google Drive integration they separately connect and a Drive folder they choose. Workspace setup also creates configuration and session-index records.

Your responsibilities

You choose what to save and are responsible for having permission to use the content, selecting and managing your Google Drive workspace, and controlling access and sharing permissions there. Do not use MekDaa to store passwords, access tokens, or information that you are not authorized to store. Check the resulting record and its coverage note before relying on it.

Service limitations

MekDaa cannot guarantee recovery of unsaved conversations, a full transcript, uninterrupted availability, background capture, or automatic recognition of your Drive folder across separate ChatGPT conversations. It uses the capabilities of ChatGPT and the Google Drive integration available to you; these services have their own rules, availability, terms and privacy practices. The publisher does not operate an independent session storage service.

Availability

MekDaa is offered without a fee by its publisher. It may change as ChatGPT, Google Drive, or the package is updated. No particular service level or continuous availability is promised. Nothing in these terms limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Questions and support
